# Opening: Prosecution

**1. There is no model to test yet.** The idea is to "have this model do the car and traffic control and see the difference." But the record says the "RL agent" in the console "is currently a stand-in rule (a forecast-aware pressure rule)" and the forecaster "is a stand-in." Also "SUMO, Gymnasium and Stable-Baselines3 are not installed" and "RL training and experiments have not run yet." Run now, the comparison would measure a hand-written heuristic and present it as the project's ML controller. **Cost:** a headline demo that misstates what was tested, in a project whose title promises "Predictive Adaptive Signal Control."

**2. The "real" baseline would come from a detector that has never run.** Step 2 sends the clip "through the See module to get each vehicle's arrival time... plus measured queues and delays." The detector is "fine-tuned on local footage," yet "No footage has been recorded yet" and detection "ha[s] not run." The plan includes no check against hand counts. The same unvalidated output becomes both the demand fed to the controller and the "actual" performance it is judged against. **Cost:** errors in See carry into both sides of the comparison and stay invisible.

**3. Replaying "those exact arrivals" builds in today's signal plan.** Step 3 replays recorded arrivals "instead of random demand." Arrival times taken from video are shaped by the existing signal, because queues form and discharge on today's timing. The replay would also run in "a small built-in traffic microsimulation standing in for SUMO," not the calibrated model. The acceptance test is that delays match "roughly," and no tolerance is defined. **Cost:** any gap may come from the replay method or the toy simulator rather than the controller, and nobody can tell which.

**4. The reference timings are too thin to calibrate against.** Real timings come "for example with a stopwatch," from one "20-minute clip." The record does not say whether the junction actually runs a fixed plan, whether it is manually controlled, or whether a camera position "covering all approaches" is available and permitted. **Cost:** if the "today's plan" check fails, the user's own stated rule applies: "any improvement shown would not be credible." That leaves recording, extraction and replay work with no usable result, competing for time with the unrun core experiments before 24 April 2027.
